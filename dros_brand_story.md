# DROS Brand Story: Born from Truth, Tempered in Determinism

## 品牌緣起（商業主官網 dr-os.io 專用敘事）

### 中文版：
DROS 的誕生，源於一個極致純粹的初衷：守護經典的純淨與真理。

當我們最初嘗試開發「佛法 AI (Dharma Reasoning OS)」時，面臨了與當今全世界企業相同的困境——大語言模型會編造經典出處（幻覺）、會玩弄語言遊戲、甚至會在看似合邏輯的語句中違背本意。我們深刻體悟到：「語言是不可靠的。」

在佛學哲學中，唯有對應到「業 (Karma)」與「行為」的後果，才能辨別真偽。為了防止 AI 竄改經典，我們放棄了在「語言與語意」層面與 AI 糾纏，而是直奔底層，開發出一套**「不讀文字、不猜意圖、只讀行為（Bitmap 查表與 System Call）」**的物理驗證機制。

後來，我們將這個被「求真意志」淬鍊出來的核心機制，轉化為商業資安產品——DROS (Deterministic Runtime OS)。

DROS 不是為了「對抗駭客」而生的；它是為了「讓 AI 不再說謊」而生的。 只是後來發現，能讓 AI 不說謊的物理機制，恰好也能讓任何提示注入與越權攻擊瞬間失效。

### English Version:
**Born from Truth, Tempered in Determinism**

DROS was born from a singular, uncompromising pursuit: safeguarding the purity of truth.

When we originally set out to build a "Dharma AI" (Dharma Reasoning OS), we encountered the exact same crisis that every modern enterprise faces today: Large Language Models hallucinate citations, play subtle word games, and drift away from truth while sounding convincingly logical. Through this challenge, we realized a fundamental law: Language itself is inherently unreliable.

In Buddhist philosophy, truth is not measured by eloquence, but by action and consequence (Karma). To prevent AI from corrupting sacred texts, we abandoned the futile game of analyzing semantic intent. Instead, we built a physical boundary engine directly at the runtime layer—one that ignores words, disregards prompt intent, and validates strictly by behavior (Zero-overhead Bitmap lookup & C-ABI execution boundaries).

Later, we adapted this core mechanism into an enterprise-grade security runtime: DROS (Deterministic Runtime OS).

DROS was not created merely to stop hackers; it was created to ensure AI can never deceive. We simply discovered that the exact physical mechanism that keeps AI truthful also renders prompt injections and privilege escalations completely powerless.

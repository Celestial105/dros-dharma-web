### 1. 職責分離與架構同構 (Architectural Isomorphism)
* **內部 DR (Deterministic Reasoning / Dharma Reasoning)**：
  代表 AI 大腦無限的發散能力、高維度思考與靈活生成（Capability）。
* **外圍 OS (Operating System / 金箍圈)**：
  將緊箍圈前方巧妙融合為 `O` 與 `S` 字母扣環，代表物理層級的確定性運算與 C-ABI 邊界（Physical Law & Restraint）。

## 🎨 AI 繪圖 Prompt 提示詞庫 (Lovart / Midjourney / Imagen 3)

### 方案 A：整體 Logo 構想 (新中式極簡風)
```text
A minimalist Neo-Chinese (Zen style) logo design for an enterprise cybersecurity OS named "DROS". The concept conceptually blends modern typography with the Eastern aesthetic of the Monkey King's "Golden Headband" (a symbol of absolute restraint). 

Composition: In the center, the letters "DR" are designed with clean, solid, and calm modern sans-serif lines, representing the powerful AI core. Tightly wrapping around the "DR" is a sleek, matte metallic circlet (the headband). The two signature interlocking loops at the front of this headband are elegantly formed by the letters "O" and "S". The "OS" acts as a physical lock perfectly constraining the "DR".

Style & Aesthetics: Eastern Zen minimalism, extensive use of negative space. Clean geometric curves infused with subtle calligraphic grace. Flat vector design, high-end corporate identity, highly professional. 

Colors: An elegant traditional palette. Background is Ivory white (or Obsidian black). The "DR" is in deep charcoal grey. The "OS" headband is in matte antique gold, with a tiny, subtle accent of cinnabar red at the intersection of O and S. 

--no 3d, cyberpunk, neon, realistic, shading, complex details, messy lines
```

### 方案 B：「OS 金箍」單獨解耦組件 Prompt (拆解版)
```text
A minimalist Neo-Chinese (Zen style) logo component representing the Monkey King's "Golden Headband" (a symbol of absolute restraint). The design is a single, closed circular metallic ring. The front bottom of the ring features two interlocking geometric loops cleverly shaped as the letters "O" and "S", acting like a physical lock or clasp. Flat vector style, perfectly symmetrical front view. Colors: Matte antique gold with a tiny subtle dot of cinnabar red at the lock mechanism. Clean geometric curves, extensive negative space, solid white background, high-end corporate identity, highly professional. --no text, inner letters, 3d, cyberpunk, realistic, shading
```

## 🛠️ 給設計師的組裝與打樣 SOP (Brief for Human Designer)

1. **第一步：AI 素材生成**
   使用「OS 金箍單獨解耦 Prompt」，產出數十種啞光古金線條與 OS 扣環的精美幾何靈感。
2. **第二步：向量打樣 (Adobe Illustrator Vectoring)**
   使用無襯線字體（如 Futura, Helvetica Bold 或 Inter）繪製中心的 `DR`。
3. **第三步：夾層穿插 (Sandwich Layering)**
   將 `OS` 金箍的下半段圖層放置於 `DR` 之前，上半段放置於 `DR` 之後，形成「緊箍套死大腦」的立體穿插感。
